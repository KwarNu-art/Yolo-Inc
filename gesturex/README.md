> **Gesture-X** is an elite rootless jailbreak tweak engineered specifically for Apple A10 Fusion devices (iPhone 7 Plus running iOS 15.0 - 15.8.x) on **Dopamine** and **Palera1n**. It brings full, native, fluid iPhone X navigation gestures to Home-button devices, enabling you to swipe up to go home in every app, swipe and hold for app switcher, flick across the bottom to switch apps, and pull down from the top-right corner for Control Center.

---

## ⚡️ Key Features & Architecture

### 1. 📲 Universal Swipe Up to Home
* Swipe up from the bottom edge in any application (Telegram, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, Safari, Settings, etc.) to immediately dismiss the app and smoothly return to the SpringBoard Home Screen.

### 2. 🎛️ Multitasking & App Switcher
* Swipe up from the bottom and pause briefly in the center of the screen to open the modern fluid App Switcher card deck.

### 3. ⚡ Quick App Flicking
* Flick left or right across the bottom edge or home bar to instantly jump between recently opened applications without opening the App Switcher.

### 4. 📊 Top-Right Control Center
* Pull down from the top-right corner of the screen to trigger Control Center, just like on iPhone X, XS, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15.

### 5. 🔔 Top-Left Notification Center / Cover Sheet
* Pull down from the top-left or top-center of the screen to reveal Notification Center and lock screen widgets.

### 6. 💎 Bottom Home Grabber Bar (Configurable)
* Native Apple-style rounded bottom home bar affordance (`SBHomeGrabberView` / `MTLSHomeAffordanceView`).
* Toggleable option in Settings to hide the home bar for an unobstructed full-screen experience during gaming.

### 7. ⌨️ Modern Bottom Keyboard Spacing
* Elevates the iOS keyboard with the classic modern iPhone bottom inset, providing dedicated Globe and Dictation buttons underneath the spacebar for superior typing ergonomics on iPhone 7 Plus.

### 8. 🔦 Lockscreen Quick Action Buttons
* Native round buttons for Flashlight (Torch) and Camera directly on the Lock Screen.

### 9. 💎 On-Screen Proof Banner
* Upon installation and every boot or respring, Gesture-X displays a floating glassmorphic banner at the top of the screen confirming that fluid gestures are hooked and active.

## 📲 Installation Guide

### Rootless Jailbreak (Dopamine / Palera1n)
1. Download **`GestureX-Rootless-arm64.deb`** from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/KwarNu-art/Gesture-X/releases).
2. Open the file in **Sileo**, **Zebra**, or **Filza**.
3. Tap **Get / Install** and confirm.
4. The post-install script automatically restarts SpringBoard.
5. You will immediately see the **📲 Gesture-X Online** proof banner on your screen!
6. Open any application and swipe up from the bottom edge to go Home!
